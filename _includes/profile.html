As a control engineer, I enjoy turning theory into real-world systems. I'm especially drawn to machines that move and interact with their environment — like drones, robots, and autonomous vehicles. I like working across the full pipeline: from modeling and control design to embedded implementation and hands-on testing. Whether it's tuning a nonlinear controller or chasing down a bug, I find joy in making complex things work cleanly and reliably.

Away from equations and code, you'll likely find me strumming an acoustic guitar (with varying success) or out exploring new places on my motorcycle - preferably with good weather and even better views.
