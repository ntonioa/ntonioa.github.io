Nonlinear predictive control of the continuum and hybrid dynamics of a suspended deformable cable for aerial pick and place

Duration: Fall 2024 - Summer 2025 (expected)

For my master's thesis, I developed a four-part control framework:

  1. a hybrid PDE-based model of a deformable cable suspended from a UAV, with a possible load at the distal end;
  2. a reduced-order model (ROM) capturing the dominant dynamics;
  3. a hybrid MPC based on the ROM to control the UAV-cable-load system;
  4. a planner that exploits the reduced dynamics to generate feasible trajectories under dynamic and state constraints.
