Research Engineer (Research Fellow) @ Sapienza

Duration: Summer 2025 - Present

For the SOAR-TOUCH project, I investigate how soft optical tactile sensing can enable energy-efficient and stable interactions for aerial robots under external disturbances. My work focuses on designing control strategies and integrating tactile perception on a hexarotor platform, with extensive simulations and hands-on experiments. This research bridges advanced control theory, machine learning, and embodied interaction to tackle real-world aerial manipulation challenges.
